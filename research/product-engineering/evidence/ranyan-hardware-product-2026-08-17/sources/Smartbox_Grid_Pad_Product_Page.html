Our 10-inch, 13-inch and 16-inch Grid Pads provide communication for every access method

The Grid Pad range is our most powerful AAC solution. Designed to help you communicate all day, every day.

 

Available in a choice of three sizes and ready for use with any type of alternative access technology, Grid Pad can be personalised to suit your individual needs.

Explore our range of devices, available with Grid AAC software

Grid Pad Go

A portable and durable 10-inch communication aid suitable for touch, keyguards, and switches to access Grid.

Explore Grid Pad Go

Grid Pad 13

A powerful 13-inch device with programmable Smart Buttons, ideally suited for wheelchair mounting or a stand using eye gaze.

Explore Grid Pad 13

Grid Pad 16

Our largest Grid Pad with a 16-inch WQXGA HDR display for users who need a wide screen size for more precise selections.

Explore Grid Pad 16
